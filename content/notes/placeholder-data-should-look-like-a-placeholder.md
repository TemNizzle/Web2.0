A useful shortcut when validating an idea: ship the guess, but make the guess
visibly a guess.

In Sun Chaser, patios without verified orientation carry an `exposureSource` of
`estimated` and render with a dashed, muted treatment instead of the solid one.
It let the map go live months before the data was complete, without misleading
anyone — and it made the remaining work legible, because the placeholders were
the to-do list.

The discipline is to never let a placeholder quietly graduate into looking
authoritative just because you got used to seeing it. See
[[trust-indicators-beat-precision|trust indicators beat precision]].
