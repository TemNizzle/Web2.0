When a number on screen can't be trusted completely, the honest move is to say so
— right next to the number, not buried in a methodology page nobody opens.

Sun Chaser shows an "Estimated" tag on any patio whose orientation hasn't been
verified in person. It costs a little polish and buys a lot of credibility: a
visitor who sees the app admit uncertainty in one place trusts the confident
numbers more everywhere else.

The failure mode on the other side is a product that presents a guess with the
same visual weight as a measurement. Users find the seams eventually, and when
they do they stop trusting the whole surface.
